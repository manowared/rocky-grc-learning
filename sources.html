Where Rocky's learning content comes from

This page identifies the primary and authoritative materials used to develop Rocky's current SOX/ICFR foundations module and its introductory access-management scenario. Each source entry explains its role and limitations.

Last source review: November 3, 2026. Links and standards should be rechecked before relying on them for professional work.

How to read this page: A source can be a law, regulation, regulator interpretation, auditing standard, or voluntary framework. They have different authority and audiences. Rocky's plain-language explanations are educational summaries, not quotations or substitutes for the original material.

Primary sources

SEC · Regulatory guidance

SEC Release No. 33-8810 — Commission Guidance Regarding Management's Report on ICFR

Supports the lesson's discussion of management's top-down, risk-based evaluation of ICFR, identifying financial-reporting risks, and selecting controls for evaluation.

Useful areas: management evaluation approach, identifying risks to reliable financial reporting, controls, and documentation.

SEC · Staff guidance

SEC Staff FAQ — Management's Report on ICFR

Supports the distinction that management retains ultimate responsibility for assessment, documentation, and testing of the company's ICFR.

Useful area: Question 17, management's ultimate responsibility; Question 18, sources of guidance available to management.

Read the SEC staff FAQ ↗
PCAOB · Auditing standard

PCAOB AS 2201 — An Audit of Internal Control Over Financial Reporting

Supports the module's explanation of the external auditor's role, reasonable assurance, top-down audit approach, control testing, and evaluation of identified deficiencies.

Useful areas: paragraphs .01–.07 (purpose and integrated audit), .21 onward (top-down approach), .42 onward (testing controls), and .62 onward (evaluating deficiencies).

Audience caveat: AS 2201 governs the auditor's work. It is not a management how-to manual. Rocky uses it to explain the auditor side of ICFR and keeps management responsibilities separate.

Read PCAOB AS 2201 ↗
COSO · Voluntary framework

COSO — Internal Control

Provides the framework context for internal control concepts and the five components of internal control. The SEC recognizes COSO as an example of a suitable, recognized framework for management's ICFR evaluation.

Use in Rocky: conceptual framework context; Rocky does not reproduce proprietary COSO framework text or claim that its short explanations replace the full framework.

Editorial policy

How Rocky handles sources and accuracy

1. Traceable claims

Substantive claims in lessons should cite a source at the point where the concept is taught. This page provides the source register; lesson pages should also identify the relevant section, paragraph, control family, or question.

2. Authority labels

Rocky labels sources as law/regulation, regulator guidance, auditing standards, voluntary frameworks, or educational interpretation. A framework or industry practice is not automatically a legal requirement.

3. No false equivalence

SOX/ICFR requirements, PCAOB auditor standards, COSO framework concepts, and NIST security controls serve different purposes. Rocky explains how they relate without implying that one replaces another.

4. Fictional practice materials

Company names, employees, logs, tickets, and workpapers in Rocky are invented for learning. They are not sourced from actual organizations and must not be presented as real audit evidence.

5. Learner work is separate

Learner answers are saved separately from validated lesson explanations and model answers. A learner's submission is never automatically promoted into instructional content.

6. Version and currency

Rocky records the date sources were reviewed and flags known effective-date changes. Always consult the official publisher for the current version and applicability.
